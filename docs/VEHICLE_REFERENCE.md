# Vehicle visual reference

The concept art depicts a compact sedan and compact SUVs because these are recognizable import-market body styles for Ghana. It is not an inventory list or a claim about an exact national popularity ranking.

Reference points consulted during design:

- [Ghana Energy Commission, Ghana Electric Vehicles Baseline Survey Report 2022](https://www.energycom.gov.gh/index.php/initiatives/drive-electric-initiative-main/dei-downloads?download=215%3Aghana-electric-vehicles-baseline-survey-report-2022) includes model shares for the narrower hybrid import segment, including Camry, Prius, and RAV4 hybrids.
- [West Coast Shipping, U.S. to Ghana vehicle market demand](https://www.wcshipping.com/blog/which-us-cars-sell-fastest-in-ghana-top-models-market-demand?hs_amp=true) discusses interest in Corolla, RAV4, and CR-V. This is a shipping company's market perspective, not an official national ranking.

The website intentionally uses generic car designs so visitors do not mistake the generated visuals for a specific vehicle Eram currently sells or rents.
