# Project brief

## Company

**Eram Shipping and Motors** serves Ghana with four offerings described by the owner:

1. Shipping cars to Ghana
2. Selling cars in Ghana
3. Renting cars in Ghana
4. Importing merchandise and selling American products in Ghana

## Website goal

Create a striking first impression and guide visitors from the car experience to the relevant business inquiry. The design uses navy and electric blue with red accents. The main scene follows a six-chapter launch-style sequence: road motion, braking, front detail, exterior form, cabin reveal, and open cargo. Visitors can switch among four views and explore the same details in a gallery with a direct call action.

## Audience paths

- **Car importer:** Understand the service and ask about shipping a vehicle.
- **Buyer:** Browse vehicle styles and ask about current stock or sourcing.
- **Renter:** Ask about dates and vehicle needs.
- **Merchandise shopper:** Ask about available imported products.

## Scope of this prototype

The experience uses layered photography, CSS perspective, scroll progress, pointer response, and view controls. It is a cinematic 3D-like presentation rather than a navigable 3D vehicle model. Accurate hinged doors and a 360-degree inspection would require licensed vehicle-specific 3D assets or a new 3D modeling phase.

## Content guardrails

The generated vehicles illustrate styles only. Do not present them as actual stock or attach a model year, price, mileage, duty status, rental rate, delivery promise, or customs claim without verified business data.
